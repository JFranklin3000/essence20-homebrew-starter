# Changelog

## Unreleased

- M.A.S.K. (in progress): a "M.A.S.K." compendium folder (Masks, Vehicles, Agents, V.E.N.O.M. Agents, Origins/Perks/
  Features, Mission Tables, Guide) and a "M.A.S.K." game line on the G.I. Joe rules, declared in `module.json`. Built
  entirely on the system's Cartoon Kit - masks are power gear (bonded, mask slot), Strike Mode is a vehicle Alt Mode -
  so the module has no M.A.S.K. scripts or macros.
- M.A.S.K. agents: six Origins (Stunt Driver, Race Driver, Inventor, Master of Disguise, Hired Gun, Tycoon), each
  granting its own Origin Perk, and six Mask Agent Perks (Signature Mask, Quick Change, Vehicle Bond, Wheel Gunner,
  Hardened Mask, Secret Identity). Mask power attacks now carry a `maskPower` roll flag for Signature Mask.
- M.A.S.K. agent roster: 17 M.A.S.K. NPCs (the agents plus Scott Trakker and T-Bob) and 11 V.E.N.O.M. NPCs (the agents
  plus a Henchman and a Wheelman), built with the Threat Builder, each wearing their bonded signature mask with their
  other masks and M.A.S.K. Perks; the guide's new Agent roster page links them with their vehicles.
- M.A.S.K. campaign pieces: Boulder Hill (the headquarters, with Command Center, Repair Bay, Hidden Hangar and Mask Lab),
  six V.E.N.O.M. heist tables in a "V.E.N.O.M. Heist" folder and the Course and Hazard tables in a "Racing Season"
  folder, each with a title and outline for the system's Mission Generator; races run on its Race and Chase Tracker.
  The guide's Running a campaign page explains both.
- The Mask Agent Role (G.I. Joe line, levels 1-20): Mask Charge Role Points and nine Role Perks of its own (Mask Surge,
  Behind the Wheel, Masked Up, Rapid Recharge, Mask Network, Steel Nerves, Evasive Driver, Mask Mastery, Living Legend),
  plus Signature Mask, Quick Change and the system's Extra Attack at their levels. Its level table is on the guide's
  Agents page.

## 1.1.0

- House rules (needs Essence20 6.0.7 or later): four examples the GM can switch on under Configure Settings → House
  Rules - Heroic Initiative, Everyone Knows Initiative, Extra General Perk (one more General Perk at level 1) and Extra
  Edges and Snags Count - in `scripts/house-rules.mjs`, ready to copy.
- The Homebrew Guide's new "House rules" page: switching them on, making your own in Foundry (New House Rule), shipping
  them in your module as rules, and the code route.

## 1.0.0

- First release: seven compendiums in a "Homebrew" folder (Perks, Powers and Spells, Roles and Origins, Gear and
  Weapons, Zord and Transformer Features, Threats and NPCs, and the Guide).
- Worked examples: a Perk with a rule and a prerequisite, a Power with an Availability, a piece of gear with a Gear Type.
- The Homebrew Guide journal: making the kit yours, what goes where, the Compendium Browser, the Rules tab,
  prerequisites, what not to include, and sharing.
