# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: seven compendiums in a "Homebrew" folder (Perks, Powers and Spells, Roles and Origins, Gear and
  Weapons, Zord and Transformer Features, Threats and NPCs, and the Guide).
- Worked examples: a Perk with a rule and a prerequisite, a Power with an Availability, a piece of gear with a Gear Type.
- The Homebrew Guide journal: making the kit yours, what goes where, the Compendium Browser, the Rules tab,
  prerequisites, what not to include, and sharing.
