# Changelog

## Unreleased

- M.A.S.K. (in progress): a "M.A.S.K." compendium folder (Masks, Vehicles, Agents, V.E.N.O.M. Agents, Origins/Perks/
  Features, Mission Tables, Macros, Guide), three house rules (Bonded Masks, Concealed Weapons, Disguised Vehicles), the
  `item:mask`, `self:maskBonded` and `self:crewWearing` rule tags with mask bonds (`scripts/mask/`), the Rebond Mask
  macro and the M.A.S.K. Guide journal. No system changes - it all runs through the public rules API.
- M.A.S.K. agents: six Origins (Stunt Driver, Race Driver, Inventor, Master of Disguise, Hired Gun, Tycoon), each
  granting its own Origin Perk, and six Mask Agent Perks (Signature Mask, Quick Change, Vehicle Bond, Wheel Gunner,
  Hardened Mask, Secret Identity). Mask power attacks now carry a `maskPower` roll flag for Signature Mask.

## 1.1.0

- House rules (needs Essence20 6.0.7 or later): four examples the GM can switch on under Configure Settings → House
  Rules - Heroic Initiative, Everyone Knows Initiative, Extra General Perk (one more General Perk at level 1) and Extra
  Edges and Snags Count - in `scripts/house-rules.mjs`, ready to copy.
- The Homebrew Guide's new "House rules" page: switching them on, making your own in Foundry (New House Rule), shipping
  them in your module as rules, and the code route.

## 1.0.0

- First release: seven compendiums in a "Homebrew" folder (Perks, Powers and Spells, Roles and Origins, Gear and
  Weapons, Zord and Transformer Features, Threats and NPCs, and the Guide).
- Worked examples: a Perk with a rule and a prerequisite, a Power with an Availability, a piece of gear with a Gear Type.
- The Homebrew Guide journal: making the kit yours, what goes where, the Compendium Browser, the Rules tab,
  prerequisites, what not to include, and sharing.
